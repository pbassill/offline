Radio channel files, installed to /usr/local/share/offline/radio.

gqrx-bookmarks.csv   Bookmarks for Gqrx: the sixteen PMR446 channels, the Meshtastic
                     EU868 frequency and a few calling frequencies. The installer copies
                     it to ~/.config/gqrx/bookmarks.csv if no bookmarks exist yet.

chirp-pmr446.csv     The sixteen PMR446 channels for CHIRP (File > Import). Narrow FM, no
                     tone. PMR446 is licence-free only for approved handsets with fixed
                     aerials at up to 0.5 W; a radio that can transmit more, or has a
                     removable aerial, is not covered even on these frequencies.
